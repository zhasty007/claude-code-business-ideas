# Claude Code Side Business Ideas

## Your edge
Small businesses can't afford big consulting firm rates, but they still need that kind of thinking: clean processes, good reporting, solid controls. You can deliver it with Claude Code for a fraction of the price. Some tooling already exists in your setup (meeting minutes, exec summaries, risk registers, market sizing, and the Invisible Expert / Ghosted Glory ghostwriting skills).

## Best ideas, roughly in order of effort and reward

1. **Back-office automation sprints ($2k to $8k fixed price)**
   Map a small business's manual work: invoicing, QuickBooks cleanup, month-end close, intake forms, scheduling. Then use Claude Code to build scripts, Zapier/Make flows, or small internal tools that automate it. Target accountants, law firms, dental/medical offices, contractors, and property managers.

2. **AI readiness assessment + implementation (consulting front door)**
   A 2 week assessment for $1.5k to $3k: where AI saves time, plus data and risk concerns. Upsell the build.

3. **Custom Claude skills and agents for professional firms (recurring revenue)**
   Build firm-specific skills like intake interviews, document summarizers, and drafting assistants (similar to the HCW law firm skills you already have). Charge a setup fee plus $300 to $1,000/month for maintenance and new skills.

4. **Productized services (scalable, less custom)**
   - Authority book + content engine for professionals (Invisible Expert is already designed)
   - Monthly financial dashboard / KPI reporting for small businesses
   - Website + SEO + review-response automation for local businesses

5. **Fractional "AI ops / FP&A" retainers**
   Act as a part-time finance and ops team: monthly close support, cash flow forecasting, board/lender reporting. Claude Code does the heavy lifting.

6. **Micro-SaaS (longer bet)**
   Once you see the same problem at 3+ clients, turn it into a product: a compliance checklist tracker, a PI case intake tool, a QuickBooks anomaly checker, etc.

7. **Teaching and content**
   Workshops for local chambers of commerce or trade groups ("AI for small business owners"), paid cohorts, and templates. This builds a lead pipeline for ideas 1 to 3.

## How to split it with 4 people
- **Sales / client lead:** discovery calls, scoping, proposals
- **Builder 1:** automations and integrations
- **Builder 2:** custom skills, agents, internal tools
- **Ops / content:** delivery QA, marketing, LinkedIn content, shared playbooks

Shared assets: one GitHub org with reusable skill templates, a standard SOW and proposal template, and a pricing sheet. Every engagement makes the next one faster.

## 90 day launch
- **Weeks 1 to 2:** Form an LLC (operating agreement, equity split, IP ownership). Pick 1 niche (e.g. small law firms or CPA firms) and 1 offer (idea 1 or 3).
- **Weeks 3 to 6:** 2 or 3 discounted or free pilots from your networks, in exchange for testimonials and case studies.
- **Weeks 7 to 12:** Move to full price, post case studies on LinkedIn, run 1 local workshop, convert pilots to monthly retainers.
- **Goal:** about $5k to $10k/month recurring by month 3. Then decide whether to productize.

## Risks
- Client data: use NDAs, keep sensitive data out of public repos, and use business-tier AI accounts with data protections.
- Scope creep: use fixed-price scopes with clear deliverables.
- Partner friction: write equity and time-commitment rules down on day one.
