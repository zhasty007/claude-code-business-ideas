## Your edge
Small businesses can't afford big consulting firm rates, but they still need that kind of thinking: clean processes, good reporting, solid controls. You can deliver it with Claude Code for a fraction of the price. Some tooling already exists in your setup (meeting minutes, exec summaries, risk registers, market sizing, and the Invisible Expert / Ghosted Glory ghostwriting skills).

## Our niche: AI for small lenders, investors, and their borrowers
The team covers IT, bond trading, and commercial lending. That points to one clear niche: **community banks, credit unions, small RIAs, loan brokers, and the small businesses that borrow from them.** These firms run on spreadsheets and PDFs, can't afford big vendors, and rarely meet anyone who understands both the finance and the tech. You do.

### Commercial lending offers
1. **Financial spreading automation.** Turn borrower tax returns, P&Ls, and balance sheets (PDFs) into standardized spreads with DSCR, leverage, and global cash flow calculated. Sell per deal or as a monthly tool to community banks, credit unions, and loan brokers.
2. **Credit memo drafting assistant.** A Claude skill that takes the spread plus borrower info and drafts a first-pass credit memo in the lender's own format. Saves analysts hours per deal.
3. **Covenant and annual review monitoring.** Borrowers upload financials, the tool calculates covenants, flags breaches, and tracks missing documents and tickler items.
4. **Loan packaging for small business borrowers.** Help businesses prepare clean SBA 7(a)/504 or conventional loan packages (projections, cash flow analysis, business plan). Brokers and banks become your referral source.

### Fixed income offers
5. **Investment portfolio reporting for credit unions and community banks.** Automated monthly reports on yield, duration, cash flow ladders, unrealized gains/losses, and rate shock scenarios for ALCO meetings.
6. **Bond analytics tools for small RIAs and family offices.** Muni and Treasury ladder builders, maturity and call schedules, and portfolio cash flow projections built from their holdings files.
7. **Market commentary engine.** Turn rates and credit market data into weekly client-ready commentary that advisors can send under their own name.

### IT offers (also the backbone of everything above)
8. **Secure AI setup for regulated firms.** Business-tier AI accounts, data handling policies, access controls, and staff training. Banks and RIAs need this before they can use any of the tools above.
9. **Vendor due diligence readiness.** Banks will send you security questionnaires before they buy. Build a lightweight security package (policies, data flow diagrams, SOC 2 readiness) once and reuse it, then sell the same service to other small fintech vendors.

### Where the three overlap (the flagship)
A **"lending desk in a box"** for community banks and credit unions: spreading + credit memo + covenant tracking on the lending side, portfolio reporting on the investment side, all set up securely by your IT person. Charge a setup fee ($5k to $15k) plus a monthly subscription ($1k to $3k). Ten clients is a real business.

### Watch outs specific to this niche
- Stay on the tools and reporting side. Don't give personalized investment advice, place trades, or arrange loans for a fee without checking licensing rules (RIA, broker-dealer, and state loan broker requirements).
- Bank data is sensitive. Assume every client will ask how their data is stored and who can see it, and have a written answer ready.

## General small business ideas (also worth doing)

1. **Back-office automation sprints ($2k to $8k fixed price)**
   Map a small business's manual work: invoicing, QuickBooks cleanup, month-end close, intake forms, scheduling. Then use Claude Code to build scripts, Zapier/Make flows, or small internal tools that automate it. Target accountants, law firms, dental/medical offices, contractors, and property managers.

2. **AI readiness assessment + implementation (consulting front door)**
   A 2 week assessment for $1.5k to $3k: where AI saves time, plus data and risk concerns. Upsell the build.

3. **Custom Claude skills and agents for professional firms (recurring revenue)**
   Build firm-specific skills like intake interviews, document summarizers, and drafting assistants (similar to the HCW law firm skills you already have). Charge a setup fee plus $300 to $1,000/month for maintenance and new skills.

4. **Productized services (scalable, less custom)**
   - Authority book + content engine for professionals (Invisible Expert is already designed)
   - Monthly financial dashboard / KPI reporting for small businesses
   - Website + SEO + review-response automation for local businesses

5. **Fractional "AI ops / FP&A" retainers**
   Act as a part-time finance and ops team: monthly close support, cash flow forecasting, board/lender reporting. Claude Code does the heavy lifting.

6. **Micro-SaaS (longer bet)**
   Once you see the same problem at 3+ clients, turn it into a product: a compliance checklist tracker, a PI case intake tool, a QuickBooks anomaly checker, etc.

7. **Teaching and content**
   Workshops for local chambers of commerce or trade groups ("AI for small business owners"), paid cohorts, and templates. This builds a lead pipeline for ideas 1 to 3.

## How to split it with 4 people
- **Commercial lending lead:** owns the lending products, knows the workflows, sells to banks, credit unions, and brokers
- **Bond trading lead:** owns portfolio reporting and fixed income tools, sells to investment officers and RIAs
- **IT lead:** security, hosting, integrations, and vendor due diligence answers
- **Fourth partner:** sales and delivery lead (or a second builder), runs proposals, project management, and marketing

Shared assets: one GitHub org with reusable skill templates, a standard SOW and proposal template, and a pricing sheet. Every engagement makes the next one faster.

## 90 day launch
- **Weeks 1 to 2:** Form an LLC (operating agreement, equity split, IP ownership). Start with community banks and credit unions. Pick 1 offer, most likely financial spreading automation (offer 1), since it is easy to demo and saves time on every deal.
- **Weeks 3 to 6:** 2 or 3 discounted or free pilots with lenders from your networks, in exchange for testimonials and case studies.
- **Weeks 7 to 12:** Move to full price, post case studies on LinkedIn, present at a state banking or credit union league event, convert pilots to monthly retainers.
- **Goal:** about $5k to $10k/month recurring by month 3. Then decide whether to productize.

## Risks
- Client data: use NDAs, keep sensitive data out of public repos, and use business-tier AI accounts with data protections.
- Scope creep: use fixed-price scopes with clear deliverables.
- Partner friction: write equity and time-commitment rules down on day one.
